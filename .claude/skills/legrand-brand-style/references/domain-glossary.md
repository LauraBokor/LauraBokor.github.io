# Domain Glossary

Legrand is the global specialist in **electrical and digital building
infrastructures** — headquartered in Limoges, France, with operations
worldwide across residential, commercial, and industrial markets. Getting
this vocabulary right is what makes copy sound like it comes from inside
the industry rather than a generic tech/marketing voice.

## Core product families

- **Wiring devices** — switches, outlets/sockets, dimmers, USB charging
  outlets, and the plates/finishes around them. The most consumer-visible
  category; huge range of design finishes (this is where interior
  design/aesthetics language is appropriate).
- **Cable management** — trunking, conduit, cable trays, floor boxes,
  raceways: the physical infrastructure that routes power and data through
  a building, usually invisible once installed. Copy here is about
  organization, safety, flexibility for future changes, and installer
  efficiency.
- **Energy distribution** — panelboards, circuit breakers, surge
  protection, energy metering/monitoring. Safety- and compliance-heavy
  category; precision and reliability language matters most here.
- **Digital infrastructure & data centers** — structured cabling,
  networking cabinets, connectivity solutions for data centers and
  enterprise networks (Legrand owns/operates brands in this space,
  including Ortronics-heritage networking products). Audience skews
  technical/IT-facility.
- **Home & building automation ("smart building")** — connected switches,
  sensors, smart thermostats/lighting/security, building management
  systems. This is where the **Eliot program** sits (see below).
- **Assistive technology** — products designed for accessibility (e.g.
  nurse call systems, assistive listening, accessible controls). Directly
  ties to the "Embrace Inclusion" value — genuine product substance, not
  just messaging.

## Programs & initiatives worth knowing by name

- **Eliot** — Legrand's cross-category program for connected objects
  ("Electricity and Information of Things"). When writing about smart/
  connected products across *any* product family, Eliot is the umbrella
  term Legrand uses — don't invent a different program name.
- Sustainability/CSR commitments are typically framed around
  electrification, energy efficiency, low-carbon buildings, and inclusion
  — consistent with the "Seize Future" and "Embrace Inclusion" values.
  Don't cite specific percentages/targets unless they were given to you;
  reference the *themes* generically if no figure is provided.

## Audiences (map to voice-and-tone.md registers)

- **Installers/electricians** — install and maintain the products day to day.
- **Architects & specifiers** — choose product lines for a building project; care about codes, certifications, design fit, and long-term performance.
- **Distributors/channel partners** — sell Legrand product lines to installers and contractors.
- **Facility managers/building owners** — operate and maintain buildings over their lifecycle; care about energy costs, reliability, and upgrade paths.
- **Consumers/homeowners** — end users of smart-home and wiring-device products.
- **Internal stakeholders** — employees, and (in more formal contexts) investors/press.

## Terminology notes

- Say "wiring devices," not "outlets and switches" as a category label (the latter is fine in plain-language homeowner copy, but "wiring devices" is the correct trade/industry term).
- Say "specifiers" for architects/engineers who choose products for a project — a standard industry term, not jargon to avoid.
- Prefer "connected" over "smart" in more technical/trade registers; "smart home"/"smart" is fine and expected in consumer-facing copy.
- "Building infrastructure" (not "building materials" or "construction supplies") is the correct category framing for Legrand's business as a whole.
